# Code of conduct

Monaged follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **Be welcoming.** People of every background and level of experience are welcome here.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, or publishing others' private information.
- **Be constructive.** Give and take feedback gracefully; focus on what's best for the project and its users.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately through GitHub (the project's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form also reaches the maintainers confidentially), or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who keep doing so.
